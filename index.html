Where we start

We start by monitoring iron and vitamin B12.

Vitamin B12

Essential for your brain and your DNA.

Vitamin B12 keeps your thinking clear and helps your body make DNA. When it runs low you can feel tired, foggy, low in mood, or tingling in your hands and feet. And being low for too long can cause irreversible damage.

37%

of adults are borderline low in vitamin B12

Iron

Essential for breathing.

Iron collects oxygen from your lungs and carries it to every cell in your body. Low iron can leave you exhausted, breathless and foggy.

women in Australia have insufficient iron

I grew up in Argentina and moved to Australia at 18 to follow a pull toward scientific innovation. I chose a double degree in biotechnology and finance on purpose: enough science to innovate, and enough commercial grounding to make sure that innovation actually reaches people.

I started my career in a lab, then spent 6+ years in consulting, helping businesses scale across industries, including pharma, healthtech, telehealth and manufacturing. I've also been a commercial advisor to startups in the medtech space.

All along I was looking for ways to support innovation in the space: helping others build their ideas, and considering building my own. A few came close, like a diagnostic app powered by computer vision and a rapid at‑home flu test. None felt personal enough.

Then my own health changed, and my frustration with the current system grew. That's when I knew what the next ten to twenty years were for, and I started my magma.
