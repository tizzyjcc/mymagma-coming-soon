# The bar: what makes superpower.com look high-end

Reference: https://superpower.com, captured 2026-09-29 at 1440×900 and 390×844 (scroll-by-scroll).
Each line below is a mechanism a critic can check by looking at a screenshot.

1. **One photo fills each story screen, edge to edge, and the text sits inside it.**
   The image is full-bleed (touches both viewport edges) and composed with empty space
   on one side. The heading and body sit in that empty space with no card, box or scrim
   behind them. Check: no text container with a visible background on any image section.

2. **The photo melts into the page.** Where a photo meets the white page there is no hard
   edge or box line. The photo's own background is white or near-white at that edge, or
   it fades into it. Check: zoom the boundary; there is no visible rectangle edge.

3. **Three type sizes per screen, headlines light and tight.** Each screen uses at most
   three text sizes. Section headlines are 30–45px, weight 400, tight letter-spacing,
   and never longer than 2 lines. The body copy is 2–3 lines max under the headline.
   Check: count distinct text sizes per screenshot; count headline lines.

4. **Pinned scroll story with a numbered index.** One section stays pinned while 3–4
   steps advance. A small right-aligned index (01, 02, 03, 04) shows the active step at
   full contrast and the rest faded. Check: consecutive scroll screenshots show the same
   frame with a different active index item and changed image/text.

5. **Colour comes from the photography, not the UI.** UI elements are neutral (white,
   near-black, greys). The warm colour on screen comes from the images (golden light,
   warm skin, amber rooms). The accent colour appears on at most one element per screen.
   Check: count accent-coloured UI elements per screenshot.

6. **Questions are asked as a stack of white pills.** The "what brings you here" quiz is
   a short headline, a one-line prompt, then 4–5 full-width white pill buttons stacked
   vertically, one answer per pill, sitting directly on a warm full-bleed photo.
   Check: options are pills, stacked, same width, no radio buttons or checkboxes.

7. **Proof is shown as big numbers with a small source.** Stats appear as 2–3 large
   numbers in a row, each with a two-line label underneath and one small source line
   below the row. Check: every number on the page has a visible source in the same view.

## Where my magma deliberately differs from the bar
- Superpower uses dark, moody photos and a black hero. my magma never uses black or dark
  backgrounds: its photos are warm, bright and golden-hour, and text sits on their light areas.
- Superpower's accent is coral #fc5f2b. my magma's accent is crimson #C02035. Orange and
  rose appear only inside the magma visual.
- The magma visual (hero and the flow between sections) has no equivalent on Superpower.
  It follows the brand guidelines section 4.3 and 8.2, not this bar.
