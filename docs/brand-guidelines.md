# my magma — Brand Base Guidelines

Version: 1.0 — August 2026. Status: Confirmed base. Owner: On The Inside Pty Ltd.
Source: supplied by the founder in the Claude Code session on 2026-09-29. Items marked [PENDING] are not decided; do not invent them.

## 1. Names
- Consumer brand: **my magma** — always lowercase, two words. Never "My Magma", "MyMagma", "MY MAGMA", "mymagma" (for the brand).
- Device name: **mymagma**.

## 2. Strategy
- Purpose: To make the invisible visible — so that what's slowly changing inside you never comes as a surprise. A peace-of-mind mission, not wellness optimisation.
- Mission: To surface what's shifting in your body before it limits what you can do.
- Vision: A world where your health is never the reason you hold back.
- Values: 1) Personal truth over population average. 2) Quiet knowing over anxious monitoring. 3) Continuous over episodic. 4) Accessible to everyone, not just the optimisers. 5) Honest about what we do and don't know (we show trends, flag signals, do not diagnose).
- Positioning: the health monitor that shows you your trend, not just a number; levels judged against your personal range, not population averages.
- Tagline: "What's happening below the surface." Full stop intentional; never a question mark.

## 3. Voice
- Personality: the friend who happens to be a brilliant scientist. Quietly intelligent, warmly precise, reassuring not alarming, deeply personal ("you", never "users"/"patients"), unhyped (no "revolutionary", "game-changing", "supercharge").
- Not: biohacker, cheerful wellness brand, clinical system, tech startup.
- Three words: Calm. Factual. Quietly encouraging. Never: alarming, preachy, vague, hyped.
- Principles: specific not vague; personal not clinical; explain every term inline on first use; encouraging not alarming; honest about limits ("my magma flags trends — your GP confirms"); concise.
- Alerts: describe what is observed, offer a next step, never diagnose. Never use: critical, deficiency detected, abnormal, warning, danger.

## 4.3 Magma visual
- AI-generated flowing magma / branching veins; the brand's primary visual.
- Full-bleed (hero, dividers) or a thin strip (~80–100px) with an organic, irregular edge; never a wave, banner or geometric curve.
- Looks like looking down at flowing lava, not a volcano stock photo. No black tones; darkest value is deep red.

## 5. Typography [open to suggestions]
- Satoshi (Fontshare; commercial licence [PENDING]) for everything: Display/H1 Bold 700, H2/H3 SemiBold 600, Body Regular 400, Label Medium 500 uppercase tracked, Caption Regular 400.
- IBM Plex Mono for biomarker values (Medium 500) and data labels (Regular 400).
- Sentence case always (labels may be uppercase). Max two typefaces. Data values larger than their context text. Body line length 60–75 characters.

## 6. Logo
- Wordmark "magma", lowercase, ultra-rounded, extremely heavy display face (as on the current site). Crimson on white, or white on crimson / on the magma visual. Satoshi is not the wordmark face.

## 7. Contrast and boilerplate
- vs fitness wearables: "Deep biomarker data. Not just fitness."
- vs blood tests: "Biomarker data. Without the blood test." Short line: "No test. No appointment. No waiting."
- One sentence: my magma is a wearable biosensor that continuously monitors your blood biomarkers — building your personal baseline so you see what's changing before you feel it. Starting with B12.
- 50 words: my magma continuously monitors the blood biomarkers that shape how you feel every day — starting with B12. A wearable device builds your personal reference range over time, so changes are measured against you, not a population average. See the trend before you feel it. No appointment. No waiting room.
- Elevator pitch: "When you feel healthy, you don't book a GP. You're busy, it feels unnecessary … No appointment. No waiting room."

## 8. Imagery
- Website hero reference (approved): horizontal band of flowing magma across the middle third of a pristine white 16:9 frame, entering left edge to right edge; photoreal molten lava from above, viscous, with hair-thin golden fissure lines; crimson at left → vivid orange-red middle → warm rose/pink at right, luminous; no black; organic irregular edges with bubble-like protrusions; warm, alive, approachable, not dangerous.
- Photography: warm, intimate, real, natural golden-hour light; people in everyday moments (reading, cooking, running in a park); device reads as comfortable jewellery. Avoid hospitals, clinical light, posed smiling at phones, stock-photo energy, model-like people, stethoscopes/white coats. Diverse across age, body type, ethnicity, life stage.
