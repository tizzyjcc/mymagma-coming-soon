# Image plan and prompts (generate in ChatGPT)

Direction (founder, 2026-09-29): like Superpower, the product is the hero and people are
secondary. The product for now is the my magma app on a phone. The mymagma device is never shown.
No faces: hands, forearms, people from behind or cropped below the chin. Scenes are at home or
in active moments. Every image matches the copy it sits beside.

Workflow for app shots: generate the scene with a phone showing a plain black screen (A), then
generate the app screen on its own (B). Claude composites B onto A's screen so the UI is sharp.

## Scene style block (prefix for every scene prompt)
Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph, 3:2 landscape, highest quality. Warm, real, lived-in. Natural golden-hour light, soft and bright, never dark or moody. Warm cream, amber and soft rose tones; no blue, green or black tones except the phone. NO FACES: only hands and forearms, or a person cropped below the chin or seen from behind. Candid, not posed. No hospitals, no medical equipment, no text, no logos, no smartwatch or wristband. The subject sits in the right half of the frame; the left 40% is soft, bright, out-of-focus space with no detail, so text can be placed on it.

## Phone rule (add to every scene with a phone)
A modern smartphone with thin bezels is the hero of the image: sharp, in focus, clearly readable size. Its screen is completely plain matte black, facing the camera almost straight on (tilted no more than 15 degrees), with no reflections, glare, fingers or objects covering it.

## App screen style block (prefix for every screen prompt)
A single mobile app screen, flat UI design, portrait 9:19.5, shown straight on with no phone frame, no hand, no background. Clean white background, generous white space, warm near-black text (#1A1212), one accent colour only: crimson #C02035 for the trend line and one highlight. Modern geometric sans-serif for words, monospace for numbers. Soft rounded cards with 16px corners and pale rose (#F0E0E0) hairline outlines. No blue, no green, no icons other than simple line icons. Calm and premium, like a well-designed health app from a top design studio. All text must be spelled exactly as written below.
